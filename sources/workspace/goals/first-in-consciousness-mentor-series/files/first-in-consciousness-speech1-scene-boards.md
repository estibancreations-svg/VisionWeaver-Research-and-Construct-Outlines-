### Scene 1 — Hook (under 8 seconds)
- **VISUAL:** Single shot on Okta, mid-river light behind her.
- **VOICEOVER (Okta):** "Stop measuring your life from the bank you left."
- **ON-SCREEN TEXT:** STOP MEASURING FROM THE BANK
- **AUDIO:** Brass bell sounds once (audio signature at the open). Music 9–12 dB under voice.
- **CAMERA:** Static, close. No branding opener.

### Scene 2 — Story: arrival
- **VISUAL:** Traveller arrives at the crossing, pack loose, looking across, then back.
- **VOICEOVER (Okta):** "She came to me at the crossing, this traveller, with her pack half undone and her breath gone. Not from the river. From the watching."
- **ON-SCREEN TEXT:** None
- **AUDIO:** Music 9–12 dB under voice.
- **CAMERA:** Slow push-in as she enters frame.

### Scene 3 — Story: the comparison
- **VISUAL:** Traveller watches two distant figures reach the far shore and a house beyond it.
- **VOICEOVER (Traveller):** "That one crossed in a morning. That one has already built a house on the far shore. I have been in this water — how long have I been in this water?"
- **ON-SCREEN TEXT:** None
- **AUDIO:** Music 9–12 dB under voice.
- **CAMERA:** Gentle pan following her gaze to the far shore, then back to her feet.

### Scene 4 — Story: the long walk
- **VISUAL:** Close on Okta's staff — rings, brass bell. Bell does not ring; she is walking, not stopped.
- **VOICEOVER (Okta):** "I have walked a long time. One ring on my staff for every hundred years, and the bell has heard this speech before — from kings, from children, from my own mouth, once, when I was young enough to hurry."
- **ON-SCREEN TEXT:** None
- **AUDIO:** Music 9–12 dB under voice. Bell silent in this scene.
- **CAMERA:** Slow tilt up the staff to Okta's face.

### Scene 5 — Story: the test
- **VISUAL:** Okta stops walking. Traveller looks down at her own feet.
- **VOICEOVER (Okta):** "So I did not answer her question. I asked her one. 'Where are your feet?'"
- **ON-SCREEN TEXT:** WHERE ARE YOUR FEET?
- **AUDIO:** Music 9–12 dB under voice.
- **CAMERA:** Camera settles and holds. Pause after the question.

### Scene 6 — Story: the answer
- **VISUAL:** Both standing in the water. Traveller half-smiles at being caught.
- **VOICEOVER (Traveller, then Okta):** "'In the river,' she said, as if I were simple. 'And where does a crossing happen,' I said, 'from the bank — or from the feet?'"
- **ON-SCREEN TEXT:** None
- **AUDIO:** Music 9–12 dB under voice.
- **CAMERA:** Two-shot, river-paced drift sideways with the current.

### Scene 7 — Story: the laugh
- **VISUAL:** Traveller laughs, shakes her head, looks at her own feet in the water.
- **VOICEOVER (Okta):** "She laughed then, a wet, tired laugh. Because she knew. She had been standing in the middle of her own life, doing the bravest part of it, and calling it behind — because she was measuring from a shore she was no longer standing on."
- **ON-SCREEN TEXT:** None
- **AUDIO:** Music 9–12 dB under voice.
- **CAMERA:** Hold on the laugh. Let the moment breathe before the reframe.

### Scene 8 — Reframe: nobody crosses from the bank
- **VISUAL:** Wide shot: the bank behind them, the river ahead. Both turn from the bank to face the crossing.
- **VOICEOVER (Okta):** "Hear me. Nobody crosses from the bank. The bank is where the crossing was decided. It is not where it is done. The crossing is done here — in the cold part, in the slow part, in the part where the far shore looks no closer than it did yesterday."
- **ON-SCREEN TEXT:** NOBODY CROSSES FROM THE BANK
- **AUDIO:** Music 9–12 dB under voice.
- **CAMERA:** Slow pull-back to the wide. Pacing reset.

### Scene 9 — Reframe: comparison is a trick
- **VISUAL:** Far-shore house in soft focus, then focus returns to the traveller's middle — her hands, her pack, her feet.
- **VOICEOVER (Okta):** "Comparison is a trick of measurement. It takes your middle — your real, wet, working middle — and lays it against somebody else's far shore, and calls the distance failure. But their shore is not your measurement. It never was. You did not see their middle. You did not stand in their water."
- **ON-SCREEN TEXT:** None
- **AUDIO:** Music 9–12 dB under voice.
- **CAMERA:** Rack focus from far shore back to mid-river.

### Scene 10 — Reframe: the punch line
- **VISUAL:** Water moving around both pairs of feet. Golden light on the current.
- **VOICEOVER (Okta):** "Mid-river, you are — not behind. Mid-river is not a falling-short. Mid-river is the address of everyone who ever crossed. The Current does not carry the ones who left. It carries the ones still standing in it."
- **ON-SCREEN TEXT:** MID-RIVER, NOT BEHIND
- **AUDIO:** Music 9–12 dB under voice.
- **CAMERA:** Low, slow shot at water level. Long pause after the punch line.

### Scene 11 — Practice: one task, at river pace
- **VISUAL:** Traveller sets her pack down on a rock, chooses one thing, begins it slowly by the river.
- **VOICEOVER (Okta):** "So today, one thing. One task — you choose it — done at river pace. No clock on it. No second task riding on its back. No measuring it against how fast it went for anyone else. Feet in the water, one task, whole attention, river pace. When the old voice says behind, you answer it with your feet: mid-river — and you keep standing in the doing. One task. That is all. The river does the rest, the way it has always done it: under you, around you, carrying what you cannot carry, for exactly as long as the crossing takes."
- **ON-SCREEN TEXT:** ONE TASK. RIVER PACE.
- **AUDIO:** Music 9–12 dB under voice.
- **CAMERA:** Steady, unhurried single take. No cuts that hurry her.

### Scene 12 — Payoff: peace is a pace (70–85% of runtime)
- **VISUAL:** Both sitting on the near shore as light deepens. The far shore no longer holds the traveller's gaze.
- **VOICEOVER (Okta):** "Peace is not the far shore. Peace was never waiting for you at the end of the crossing, holding a dry towel and a certificate. Peace is a pace. Not a place. And a pace can be chosen here — mid-river, mid-life, mid-everything — today."
- **ON-SCREEN TEXT:** PEACE IS A PACE, NOT A PLACE
- **AUDIO:** Music 9–12 dB under voice.
- **CAMERA:** Slowest camera of the episode. Hold after "Not a place."

### Scene 13 — Bridge: hurry feels like safety
- **VISUAL:** Traveller drying her feet on the shore. Okta stands, bell rings once at the bridge, and she walks on.
- **VOICEOVER (Traveller, then Okta):** "But I will tell you what she asked me next, this traveller, drying her feet on my shore. She said: 'Then why does hurry feel so much like safety?' Ah. That — that is next week's teaching. Hurry has a reason, and the reason has a name. Walk at river pace until then."
- **ON-SCREEN TEXT:** NEXT — HURRY IS FEAR WITH ITS SHOES ON
- **AUDIO:** Brass bell rings once at the bridge. Music 9–12 dB under voice.
- **CAMERA:** Gentle follow as Okta walks out of frame. Bridge outro — no hard goodbye, no end-card branding block.

## Part 3 — Camera work / shot-flow diagrams

Each diagram below is a small top-down or side-view sketch description: the camera path is drawn as an arrow, the subject as a fixed mark, and the move is named. A future board pass may replace these text diagrams with drawn ones; the moves and directions must not change without a board update.

- **S1 — Static close (locked-off):** Camera mark fixed, arrow: none. Subject (Okta) centred, mid-river light behind. No move.
- **S2 — Slow push-in:** Camera mark on the near bank, straight arrow toward the traveller entering frame. Slow, unhurried.
- **S3 — Gentle pan:** Camera mark fixed on the bank; curved arrow left→right following the traveller's gaze to the far shore, then back right→left to her feet.
- **S4 — Slow tilt up:** Camera mark low at the staff; vertical arrow bottom→top, staff rings → bell → Okta's face.
- **S5 — Settle and hold (locked-off):** Camera mark fixed; arrow: none after a short settle. Hold through the question and the pause.
- **S6 — River-paced drift:** Camera mark beside both figures; sideways arrow moving with the current, same speed as the water.
- **S7 — Hold (locked-off):** Camera mark fixed on the laugh. No move; let the moment breathe.
- **S8 — Slow pull-back:** Camera mark near the pair; straight arrow away from them to the wide shot, bank behind, river ahead.
- **S9 — Rack focus (no camera move):** Camera mark fixed; focus arrow: far-shore house (soft) → traveller's hands/pack/feet (sharp). The camera does not travel; the focus does.
- **S10 — Low slow water-level:** Camera mark at water level; short slow arrow drifting with the current past both pairs of feet.
- **S11 — Steady single take (locked-off):** Camera mark fixed on the traveller at the rock. No cuts.
- **S12 — Slowest camera (locked-off or near-locked):** Camera mark fixed on both seated figures. Hold after "Not a place."
- **S13 — Gentle follow:** Camera mark behind Okta; arrow following her as she walks out of frame. Bridge outro.

## Part 4 — Lighting / mood notes

- **Overall:** Warm golden hour throughout, soft highlights, readable shadow detail. Faces never blown out or crushed.
- **S1–S3:** Mid-river light behind Okta; traveller lit from the side, slightly cooler, so the contrast between her hurry and Okta's calm reads in the light.
- **S4–S7:** Closer, warmer light; the staff and bell catch small highlights; the laugh (S7) is the warmest beat of the story section.
- **S8–S10:** Wide golden light on the current; low water-level glow in S10; the far shore softens while the mid-river water stays sharp and bright.
- **S11:** Steady even light; nothing hurries the eye.
- **S12:** Light deepens; the far shore dims while the near shore stays warm — the payoff is lit as arrival, not as a new location.
- **S13:** Light holds as Okta walks out; no dimming to black, no end-card block — the bridge stays open into Speech 2.
