## Series

**Fireside Chats** — pilot of the factory line (TYPE → PIECES → LINE → SCHEDULE → POST).

## Subject matter in one line

Grand tells the story: warm, fireside storytelling vignettes — an elder narrating, the room listening, the fire talking between the words.

## LOOK

- **Style:** photorealistic, cinematic. Locked photoreal look per the world bible.
- **Palette:** firelight golds, ember oranges, deep warm browns, soft amber shadows; lamplight pools on rugs and wood.
- **Lighting:** practical-source lighting — fireplace, lamps, the glow of the old radio. No flat studio light.
- **Camera language:** slow, unhurried. Gentle push-ins, static wide shots, drifting macro on flame and embers. Nothing cuts fast.
- **Locked reference stills:** Grand's locked still — upload as the app's reference image; the image becomes the clip's opening frame, and the scene description says what happens from that frame. Set stills: Grand's Room, Radio Corner.

## FEEL

- **Tone:** intimate, unhurried, safe. A story being told *to you*, not at you.
- **Mood:** late-night warmth; dust motes in lamplight, embers drifting, the crackle under the words.
- **Pacing:** slow. Every clip holds its breath a beat longer than feels necessary.

## VOICE

- **Narration:** Grand himself tells it — a warm, weathered elder's voice, storytelling cadence, unhurried.
- **How it's voiced (decided 2026-11-05: both, in the app):** (a) he uploads his narration audio and the app lays it under the clip, or (b) in-app text-to-voice, type the line, pick the voice, it speaks it. App voices: Elias, Mabel, Kendrick — Mabel for the grandmother.
- **Music direction:** near-none. Room tone and fire crackle only; no score over Grand's voice. If any underscoring appears, it is a single low warm tone, barely there.

## Recurring sets / characters

- **Grand's Room:** worn leather armchair beside a crackling stone fireplace; framed photos and old books on the walls.
- **Radio Corner:** vintage wooden radio glowing softly on a side table, second armchair angled toward it, warm lamplight pooling on a rug.
- **Grand:** elderly storyteller, weathered face, eyes bright, mid-story knowing smile. Each household's elder tells in their own home — the uploaded still is the clip's opening frame.

## Prompt template (master clip-prompt pattern)

From the Drive template + last night's builds: **scene-type → duration → style preset → directing rule.**

Each prompt states: (1) what the camera sees, in one shot-type; (2) the duration (5 / 8 / 10s — the app's real limits); (3) a locked preset (Cinematic Warm or Documentary Real for this series — not Animated); (4) a directing rule: one camera move or stillness, light sources named, motion named.

## Starter prompts (the five built 2026-11-03 — keep as-is for first tests)

1. **The signature.** A warm, firelit sitting room at night. An elderly storyteller in a worn leather armchair beside a crackling stone fireplace, leaning forward, hands gesturing as he speaks. Framed photos and old books on the walls. Camera slowly pushes in from wide. Cinematic Warm. *(Upload the locked Grand still as the reference image.)*
2. **Grand close-up.** Extreme close-up of the storyteller's weathered face lit by firelight, eyes bright, mid-story, a knowing smile forming. Embers drifting soft in the dark behind him. Cinematic Warm.
3. **Radio Corner.** A cozy vintage radio corner, old wooden radio glowing softly on a side table, a second armchair angled toward it, warm lamplight pooling on a rug, dust motes drifting. Slow static wide shot. Documentary Real.
4. **Fireside Kids.** A kind elderly storyteller in a soft armchair reading a big picture book to two small children on a rug, fireplace glowing warmly behind them, soft golden light, gentle and safe. Cinematic Warm.
5. **Fire B-roll.** Macro shot of flames curling around burning logs, embers pulsing orange and red, sparks rising slowly into darkness. No people. Cinematic Warm.
