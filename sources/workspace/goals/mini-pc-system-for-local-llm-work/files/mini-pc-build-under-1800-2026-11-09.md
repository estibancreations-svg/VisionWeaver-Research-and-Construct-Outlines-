# Mini PC build that fits $1,800 — prepared 2026-11-09

## What changed since last night's reply

Last night's reply said $1,800 no longer buys the big box plus a screen. That is true
for the 64GB/128GB Strix Halo boxes (GMKtec EVO-X2 64GB $2,399.99; 128GB $3,499.99–
$3,649.99; Beelink GTR9 Pro ~$4,349 — Meta Catalog listings, 2026-11-08). But a
COMPLETE working system does fit $1,800 if the box steps down a class. The overnight
reply priced only the top tier; it did not price the 32GB tier that the saved catalog
already contained.

## Build A — desk setup (recommended if he has a stable desk spot)

- **GMKtec EVO-X1, Ryzen AI 9 HX 370, 32GB LPDDR5X, 1TB SSD — $1,149.99 list**
  (GMKtec store; Amazon "new from $1,149.99" as of 11/8/2026 per starryhope;
  Notebookcheck reported Amazon sale prices of $799.98–$899.99 for this exact
  32GB/1TB config in Oct–Nov 2026, so check Amazon first — it may be well under list.)
- **Newsoul 16" 4K portable monitor, 1200 nits, USB-C — $272.97**
  (Meta Catalog listing, 2026-11-08; NOT Amazon-verified.)
- **Total: ~$1,423 at list, ~$1,073–$1,173 at the recent Amazon sale price.**
  Headroom: ~$377 at list for keyboard, mouse, USB-C hub.
- Affirm estimates for $1,423 (not a quote; APR set at Amazon checkout):
  12 mo: ~$119/mo at 0%, ~$132/mo at 20%, ~$143/mo at 36%. 24 mo at 20%: ~$72/mo.

What 32GB gets him: the HX 370 APU shares system RAM with its Radeon 890M graphics,
so it runs 8B/13B models well and ~20B-class models with room. One big model loaded
at a time — not several large models simultaneously. Dense 70B is not practical here.
**Caveat: RAM is soldered LPDDR5X — 32GB forever, no upgrade later.** Storage is
upgradeable (dual M.2 slots). If he wants a machine that grows to 64GB later, that
is the $2,400 Strix Halo box — over budget; the honest trade is 32GB now vs saving
for 64GB later.

## Build B — portable (if he has no stable desk spot)

- **MacBook Air M4 13", 24GB unified memory, 512GB SSD — ~$1,273 on Amazon US**
  (pricehistory.app tracker: $1,273.09 on 11/8/2026; lowest tracked $1,127.06.
  NOT a live Amazon check.)
- Screen + battery built in; nothing else to buy. Headroom ~$527.
- Affirm estimates for $1,273: 12 mo ~$106/mo at 0%, ~$118/mo at 20%, ~$128/mo at 36%.
- Same 24GB class as the Mac Mini M4 Pro 24GB ($1,774.95, Newegg catalog) — but the
  Mini leaves ~$25 for a screen, so it does NOT fit as a complete system. The Air
  is the Mac route that fits.

## Build C — cheapest complete desk system

- **Beelink SER9 Pro, Ryzen 7 H255, 24GB, 1TB — $819** (Best Buy, Meta Catalog)
  + Newsoul 16" 4K monitor $272.97 = **~$1,092**. Headroom ~$708.
- 24GB runs 8B models well; 13B workable. Weakest of the three for local LLMs.

## What local LLMs will NOT do (already told him; keep to one line)

They do not replace Runway video (cloud-only) or the best cloud writing models for
his books. They are for private drafts, brainstorming, and offline work.
