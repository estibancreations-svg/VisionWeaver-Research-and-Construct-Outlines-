# DECISIONS — Standing decisions governing VisionWeaver

The settled rules the work runs under. A settled item is never reported as open
again (Steve's verification standard, Oct 21 2026). All times America/Chicago.

1. **Everything runs in and through VisionWeaver** (Steve, Oct 20 2026, 23:34 CDT).
   No hand-finishing from chat. Chat produces specs, casts, and job files; the
   loop (cast → voice → animate → assemble → publish) does the work. A stalled
   stage is a system defect to repair in the system, not a step to do by hand.

2. **Small decisions are Jason's** (Steve, Oct 20 2026). Steve does not answer
   more questions or decide small things. His approval is required on big things
   only: publishing anything, spending beyond an authorized budget, deleting
   anything live, submitting anything to an outside party, changing keys or
   credentials, opening an account or signing anything. "Do XYZ" covers exactly
   what he named, nothing adjacent.

3. **The Completion Gate** (Steve, Oct 20 2026, 23:44 CDT — applies to ALL systems
   whenever work is about to be handed back as completed):
   (1) Is this what Steven requested — scope and function?
   (2) Am I comfortable saying this is the best work I can do and it meets what
   is needed? (3) If I could improve this by 150×, what would I do? — then do
   that improvement and run the review again. If still looping after the 3rd
   iteration, PAUSE and discuss with Steve. Nothing less than a full, working,
   stand-alone system ready for him to use is an acceptable hand-back.

   directive is given"): everything handed back is double- and triple-checked
   for accuracy AND applicability; every claim back-traceable to its source;

   never resurface as open. (Sparked by the ep29 v2/v3 misstatement, Oct 20.)
