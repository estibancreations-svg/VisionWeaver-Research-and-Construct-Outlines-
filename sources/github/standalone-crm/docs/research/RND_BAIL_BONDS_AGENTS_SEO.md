## Email agent plan (became T.H.E.L.M.A.'s "draft first" rule)

1. **Read-only / draft mode** — classify mail (urgent lead, follow-up, review candidate, campaign,
   vendor), draft replies, daily approval queue. Nothing sent.
2. **Approved low-risk automation** — approved templates only (acknowledgment, missing info, review
   request, internal task); log every send; urgent bail leads go phone-first to a human.
3. **Integrated agent** — CRM, call tracking, Google Business Profile; escalate legal questions,
   complaints, sensitive case details and negative reviews.

## Compliance notes (built into the Version 2.0 compliance check)

- Reviews only from real customers — no fake, AI-generated, incentivized or insider reviews (FTC rule).
- Bail-bond ads: no legal advice, no false guarantees, no claims that can't be documented.

## Access needed before an agent goes live

Google Business Profile access per location · correct inbox access · website form routing · call
tracking/CRM access · approved messaging boundaries · who approves claims, templates and Spanish copy.
