## Part A: The Conversation Starter (2-5 Minutes)
### 1. The Hook (Listen First)
"Hi! We're talking to **Poinciana** neighbors about what concerns them most. Is it our **streets/infrastructure** or **safety and emergency response**?"
### 2. Local Problems (Drill Down)
"If you had the power to fix one thing in **Poinciana**—the potholes on your street, or getting faster emergency help—what would it be?"
### 3. The Big Idea (The Pitch)
"We are exploring how **Poinciana** can get **local control** over services like roads and safety, instead of relying on faraway counties. Does that sound interesting?"
**(Simple Point: We want our local tax money to stay right here in Poinciana to fix *our* infrastructure and improve our safety and security.)**
### 4. The Ask (Get Contact Info)
"We're starting a list of people who care about this. Can I add your name and contact info so we can invite you to a short, simple meeting next month?
