	•	Creative quality began to outweigh hyper-granular audience targeting in many campaign types.
	•	Search behavior started shifting from traditional search engines toward AI assistants and conversational interfaces.
	•	Businesses increasingly adopted automation to connect CRM systems, email, advertising, analytics, and customer service.
