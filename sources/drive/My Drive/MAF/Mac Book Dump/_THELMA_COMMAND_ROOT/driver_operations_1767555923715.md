## Pre-Trip Check-In: Vehicle Inspection and System Login
Before starting any trip, drivers must complete a thorough check-in process:

### Vehicle Inspection
1. **Exterior Inspection**:
   - Walk around the vehicle and check for damages.
   - Inspect tires for proper inflation and tread depth.
   - Ensure lights, signals, and mirrors are in good working condition.

2. **Interior Inspection**:
   - Check that seatbelts and safety equipment are in place.
   - Ensure that all gauges are functioning correctly.
   - Confirm that the fire extinguisher and first aid kit are readily available.

3. **Fluid Levels**:
   - Check oil level, coolant, brake fluid, and windshield washer fluid.

### System Login
1. **Log In to Trucking Pro**:
   - Open the Trucking Pro app on your device.
   - Enter your username and password.
   - Verify your credentials by completing any two-factor authentication steps.

2. **Check Notifications**:
   - Review any alerts or messages regarding your route or loads.
   - Ensure you acknowledge and address any issues outlined in the notifications.

> **Safety Note**: Always perform these checks in a safe environment and report any issues immediately to your supervisor.

## Route Navigation: GPS Tracking and Route Optimization
### GPS Tracking
- Utilize the GPS feature on Trucking Pro to:
  - Get real-time location tracking for safety and monitoring.
  - Share your location with dispatchers and relevant stakeholders.

### Route Optimization
1. **Initial Route Setup**:
   - Input the pickup and delivery addresses into the Trucking Pro navigation system.

2. **Optimize Route**:
   - Allow the system to suggest the best route based on traffic conditions, road closures, and safety.

3. **Follow Navigation Prompts**:
   - Adhere strictly to GPS instructions while driving. Refrain from using personal navigation apps to avoid confusion.
   - Update any changes in the route promptly if rerouted by dispatch.

> **Compliance Note**: Always comply with local traffic laws and regulations during navigation.

## Load Management: Pickup, Delivery, and Confirmation Procedures
### Pickup Procedures
1. **Arrive at Pickup Location**:
   - Confirm arrival via Trucking Pro.
   - Communicate with the contact person at the pickup site.

2. **Load Verification**:
   - Check load details against the manifest.
   - Ensure the load is secure before departing.

### Delivery Procedures
1. **Arrive at Delivery Location**:
   - Notify dispatch of your arrival.
   - Follow site-specific protocols for unloading.

2. **Unload the Cargo**:
   - Ensure safety protocols are in place for unloading the cargo.
   - Confirm that the cargo matches the delivery document.

### Confirmation Procedures
1. **Complete Delivery Confirmation**:
   - Obtain a signature from the consignee.
   - Upload delivery proof via Trucking Pro.

2. **Update Load Status**:
   - Change the load status in the Trucking Pro to ‘Delivered’.

> **Compliance Note**: Ensure all paperwork is accurate and retain copies for records.

## Communication: Dispatcher Messaging and Emergency Protocols
### Dispatcher Messaging
- Use the in-app messaging feature for:
  - Daily check-ins and updates.
  - Reporting delays or issues.
  - Asking questions regarding routes or loads.

### Emergency Protocols
1. **In Case of Breakdown**:
   - Safely move the vehicle to the shoulder if possible.
   - Notify dispatch immediately through Trucking Pro.
   - Follow instructions for roadside assistance if needed.

2. **In Case of Accidents**:
   - Ensure safety and call for medical help if necessary.
   - Take photographs of the scene for documentation.
   - Inform dispatch as soon as possible.

> **Safety Note**: Always prioritize safety in communications and during emergencies, follow the chain of command and report directly to dispatch.

## Hours of Service: HOS Compliance and Logging
### HOS Compliance
- Familiarize yourself with the HOS regulations:
  - Maximum driving time without a break: 11 hours.
  - Mandatory rest periods: 10 hours off duty.
  - Weekly driving limits: 60/70 hours depending on the cycle.

### Logging your Hours
1. **Daily Log Entry**:
   - Use Trucking Pro to log your driving hours accurately.
   - Record any off-duty and sleeper berth hours appropriately.

2. **Review Logs Regularly**:
   - Conduct weekly reviews of your HOS logs.
   - Address any discrepancies immediately per compliance requirements.

> **Compliance Note**: Non-compliance with HOS regulations may lead to penalties; always maintain accurate records.
