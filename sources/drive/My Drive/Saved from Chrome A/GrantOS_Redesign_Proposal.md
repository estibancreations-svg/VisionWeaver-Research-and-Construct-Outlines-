Visual Identity: The Yellow Notepad

## Slide 3
Secondary Option: The Executive Chalkboard
Strategy Mode
• Dark Mode interface for high-focus strategy sessions.
• High-contrast 'chalk' text (White, Mint, Gold).
• Visualizing complex grant networks as board sketches.
Notepad vs. Chalkboard
Users can toggle between Notepad (Task Management) and Chalkboard (Strategy & Visualization) themes via the global settings panel.
