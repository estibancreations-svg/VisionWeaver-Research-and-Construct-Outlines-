@Zillow I have a system I’m designing for marketing properties and agent post. How can you integrate with what I want to do?
Based on everything we’ve been discussing about your ecosystem, I actually think your real estate platform is much larger than a property search website. Zillow could become one of several data and marketing sources feeding your system rather than being the system itself.
From what I understand, your vision includes:
- AI-generated property commercials
- Automated social media campaigns
- Agent branding
- Brokerage marketing
- Builder marketing
- Residential and commercial listings
- Investor packages
- Vacation rentals
- Property management
- Etsy/Pinterest marketing assets
- Grant-funded housing projects
- Photography and videography workflows
- AI content creation from a single property upload
That makes Zillow just one piece of the puzzle.
Here’s how I would architect it.
### 1. Listing Intelligence
When an agent enters a property address, your platform can retrieve:
- Property details
- Price history
- Neighborhood information
- Nearby amenities
- Maps
- Market trends
- Estimated values (where appropriate)
Instead of manually typing everything, much of the property profile can be prefilled.

- MLS descriptions

- YouTube descriptions
- Email campaigns

One property becomes dozens of marketing assets.

## Where Zillow Fits
Rather than replacing Zillow, your platform could use Zillow as one of several data sources and marketing touchpoints where permitted. Zillow’s capabilities can support:
- Property search
- Property details lookup
- Neighborhood exploration
- Estimated values (Zestimates)
- Rent Zestimates
- Mortgage affordability tools
- Mortgage payment simulations
Your system would then add the layers Zillow doesn’t provide:
- AI marketing automation
- Brand management
- Commercial generation
- Multi-platform publishing
- Creative asset production
- CRM integration
- Content library
- Performance analytics
- Workflow orchestration
I actually see this evolving into something much closer to a Real Estate Marketing Operating System (REMOS) than a traditional listing platform.
Given the architecture you’ve shared across our previous discussions, I would design it as a modular platform with engines for Property Intelligence, Creative Studio, Marketing Automation, Agent CRM, Content Publishing, Analytics, and an AI Orchestrator coordinating the entire workflow. That approach would also make it easier to add future integrations beyond Zillow, such as MLS/IDX providers, social media platforms, Adobe Creative tools, CRMs, and brokerage systems without redesigning the core platform.
